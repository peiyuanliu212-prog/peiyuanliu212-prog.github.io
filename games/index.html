How to play (same engine, all three games)

  1. Tap the big Start button.
  2. You start as a nobody. Pick your background / faction when asked.
  3. Each chapter tells a short scene, then offers you choices. Every choice changes your stats, your allies and your fate.
  4. When you meet a famous figure, you can take one of their skills and build your own mix. That's the whole game: assemble a legend out of other legends.
  5. Survive all 20 chapters to reach an ending. Your final score and ending appear on a poster you can save and share.
